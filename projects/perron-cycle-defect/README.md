# Perron defects under reciprocal edge twists

1200 · 5 November 2026 · Revision 5

[Read the paper](perron-defects.pdf) · [LaTeX source](main.tex) · [Bibliography](references.bib)

The paper proves the sharp unicyclic orders 4, 8 and 12, the two-periodic equality class, and explicit realizations of all orders 4m. It identifies the first three unrestricted minimum dimensions and the first two zero-diagonal bipartite minima. The general 2m+2 upper bound is optimal only in the stated two-cell class.

This is paper-track verification, with no Lean verification or journal acceptance. The paper distinguishes the known variational antecedents from the results studied here; priority is not asserted.
