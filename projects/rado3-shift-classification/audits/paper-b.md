# Independent paper review B: three-color shifted Schur equations

**Decision: PASS.** I checked the entire exact claim `C-857f57e728d8133faa478ad6`, revision 2, in `PKG-557bf624780f746a56e17478` (request `REQ-2901e69ad4c5e09f1c9e8c95`). This is a single paper review of this claim by `independent-sol-paper-b`. I worked in a fresh reviewer context from the sealed request, without the author or other reviewers' reasoning or prior packages. I checked all six listed material sizes and SHA-256 hashes. I read `statement.md`, the proof Markdown, `main.tex`, and both rendered pages of the sealed PDF. The PDF and TeX agree on the quantifiers, equations, table, endpoints, and proof.

**B-RADO-01 — Off-diagonal reduction and avoidance** (`main-theorem-U.md`, Off the diagonal; TeX/PDF §2). Put `m=2^s` for the stated minimum over *nonzero* differences. Each difference is divisible by `m`; at least one is not divisible by `2m`. Thus exactly two labels occupy one of the two lifts of the common residue from modulus `m` to `2m`, and one occupies the other. This includes triples with exactly two equal shifts. Permute labels and translate all three variables by `a−1`; then `u+v−w=x+y−z+a−1`, so the forbidden new shifts are `1,1,1+m` modulo `2m`. This reduction does not require the representative `1+m` to be less than `2m`.

For normalized residues, write each `hm+r` with `h` in `{0,1}`, `0≤r<m`. A monochromatic solution in color `i` would give `(H+δ)m+r_u+r_v+1−r_w ≡ 0 (mod 2m)`, where `H=h_u+h_v−h_w` and `δ=[i=2]`. The remainder expression is at least `2−m>−m` and at most `2m−1`; thus the only allowed multiples of `m` are `jm` for `j=0,1`, determined by the parity of `H+δ`. For `m=1` the expression is exactly 1. In half 0, `r ≡ i (mod 3)`; in half 1, `r ≡ 1+q−i`, where `q ≡ m (mod 3)` lies in `{1,−1}`. Substituting gives the residual expression `i+1+(1+q−2i)H−jq`. I recomputed it by testing all eight triples of halves with their actual color-compatible residues, rather than using the manuscript's expression as the check. The resulting rows for `H=−1,0,1,2` are `(1,1,2,2)`, `(1,2,1,2)`, `(2,2,1,1)` for `q=1` and `(2,1,2,1)`, `(2,2,1,1)`, `(1,1,2,2)` for `q=−1`. None vanishes. The construction tolerates empty colors and coinciding inputs. Its pullback along `x↦(x+a−1) mod 2m` avoids every integer solution, so restriction disproves every finite forcing threshold. It has period `2m`; even the least positive period is `2m`, since the halves differ at `r=0` for `q=1` and at `r=1` for `q=−1` (then `m≥2`), and any proper divisor of `2m` divides `m`.

**B-RADO-02 — Exact diagonal endpoint** (`main-theorem-U.md`, On the diagonal; TeX/PDF §3). Translation by `c` in all variables converts `x+y+c=z` to ordinary addition. For `b=c+1`, the upper test interval becomes `[b,14b]`, so the Schur triple in the coloring of indices `1..14` lifts to a triple on the multiples of `b`, and translates back inside `[1,13c+14]`. The lower test interval is `[b,14b−1]`. The floor-block indices partition as claimed. The first class is wholly `1 mod 3`, whereas adding two members and a carry of 0 or 1 gives residues 2 or 0. For the second class, all sums with either carry are in `[4,7]`, `[13,16]` or `[22,25]`, disjoint from that class; for the third, the sums start at 10. Euclidean remainders of two inputs generate only the two carries tested, including for `b=1`. This avoids all triples on `[1,13c+13]` and on each smaller interval. I independently enumerated sum-free subset partitions using a forbidden bitmask for each `x+y=z`, including its two-element support for `x=y`. Fixing the first class to contain 1, enumerating every submask of its complement as the second class, and restoring the three choices of first-class label gives 18 labelled colorings of `[1,13]` and none of `[1,14]`.

**B-RADO-03 — Sources and scope** (TeX/PDF §§3–4). [Adhikari et al., published article](https://idus.us.es/server/api/core/bitstreams/f1a1a79d-9e6f-4778-9c3d-e73ded5f7c2c/content), §§1.1 and 4.4, uses the same monochromatic convention and gives the Schur endpoint, the exact partition in Proposition 4.9, and `13c+14` for `c≥0` in Corollary 4.10, attributing it to Schaal (reference 20, MR1369349). I did not obtain Schaal's original 1995 full text; the later primary proof and independent derivation verify the diagonal fact. [Adak et al., arXiv:2603.28216v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.28216), Definition 1.2 and Theorem 1, is explicitly a two-color result. It does not supply or contradict the three-color assertion.

Independent reproduction is in [independent_check.py](independent_check.py) and [independent_check.log](independent_check.log). It also tests the cyclic class sums for all pairs of residues in every power-of-two period from 2 to 16,384. The uniform residue argument covers all remaining periods and all allowed shifts. I found no open mathematical issue. This review does not certify novelty or any formal proof.
